# Privacy Policy

Smaller, Please is built around local-first processing to ensure your media remains under your control.

## 1. Local Media Processing
Your images and videos are processed entirely on your local Mac. Smaller, Please does **not** upload your media to a Smaller, Please media-processing server. Original source files are never modified or deleted by default — Smaller, Please only writes optimized copies. The files you optimize never leave your device unless you explicitly upload them to a third-party website (such as an AI tool).

## 2. Configuration and Storage
- **Config and Cache:** Configuration files, the optimization cache, and deduplication stores are kept locally on your machine.
- **Browser-Local Data:** Extension settings, website rules, and optimization counters (bytes saved) are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`). We do not sync this data to our servers.

## 3. Telemetry and Accounts
Smaller, Please operates without user accounts, behavioral telemetry, or tracking. We do not read, record, or transmit your chat histories, browsing activities, or the contents of your images and videos.

## 4. Third-Party Integrations
- **FFmpeg / Homebrew:** The product may prompt or offer to use system or Homebrew FFmpeg for media encoding. These are local binaries and governed by their respective ecosystems.
- **Licence and Payment Services:** Licence verification is local and offline — `smaller` verifies
  a stored licence on your Mac with no network request. Payment processing for the Pro tier is
  handled by a separate payment provider (such as Paddle); it never receives your images, videos,
  or local file metadata.
